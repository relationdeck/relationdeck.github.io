# 支援

使用 RelationDeck 關係盒子遇到問題，或想提供題目建議，都歡迎聯絡我們。

## 聯絡我們

- **Email：** [relationdeck@gmail.com](mailto:relationdeck@gmail.com)，我們通常會在幾個工作天內回覆。
- **Facebook 社群：** [facebook.com/groups/relationdeck](https://facebook.com/groups/relationdeck)

回報問題時，若能附上手機型號、系統版本，以及 App 的版本（「設定 → 關於」最下方），我們能更快找到原因。

## 常見問題

- **需要註冊帳號嗎？** 不需要，下載後打開就能使用。
- **我的筆記存在哪裡？** 收藏、筆記、對話者與抽卡紀錄都只存在你的手機上，不會上傳到任何伺服器。
- **沒有網路可以用嗎？** 可以，所有卡牌都內建在 App 中。
- **每日提醒沒有出現？** 確認「設定 → 每日提醒」已開啟，且系統設定有允許 RelationDeck 傳送通知。當天已經抽過卡就不會再提醒。
- **怎麼刪除所有資料？** 在「設定 → 資料 → 清除所有資料」，或解除安裝 App。

更多問題請見[常見問題](/explore/faq)與[使用方式](/explore/how-to-use)；資料如何處理請見[隱私權政策](/privacy)。

## Support (English)

Questions, bug reports or ideas for new cards are always welcome.

- **Email:** [relationdeck@gmail.com](mailto:relationdeck@gmail.com) — we usually reply within a few business days.
- **Community:** [facebook.com/groups/relationdeck](https://facebook.com/groups/relationdeck)

When reporting a problem, please include your phone model, OS version and the app version (Settings → About).

**Common questions**

- **Do I need an account?** No. Open the app and start drawing.
- **Where are my notes stored?** Favourites, notes, people and draw history stay on your phone only. Nothing is uploaded.
- **Does it work offline?** Yes. Every card is built into the app.
- **The daily reminder doesn't show up.** Make sure Settings → Daily reminder is on and notifications are allowed for RelationDeck in your system settings. If you have already drawn a card that day, the reminder is skipped.
- **How do I delete my data?** Settings → Data → Clear all data, or uninstall the app.

See the [privacy policy](/privacy) for how data is handled.
