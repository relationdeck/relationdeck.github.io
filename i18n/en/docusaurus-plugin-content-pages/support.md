# Support

Questions, bug reports or ideas for new cards are always welcome.

## Contact us

- **Email:** [relationdeck@gmail.com](mailto:relationdeck@gmail.com) — we usually reply within a few business days.
- **Facebook group:** [facebook.com/groups/relationdeck](https://facebook.com/groups/relationdeck)

When reporting a problem, please include your phone model, OS version and the app version (at the bottom of Settings → About). It helps us find the cause faster.

## Common questions

- **Do I need an account?** No. Download it, open it and start drawing.
- **Where are my notes stored?** Saved cards, notes, people and draw history stay on your phone only. Nothing is uploaded to any server.
- **Does it work offline?** Yes. Every card is built into the app.
- **The daily reminder doesn't show up.** Make sure Settings → Daily reminder is on and that notifications are allowed for RelationDeck in your system settings. If you've already drawn a card that day, the reminder is skipped.
- **How do I delete all my data?** Go to Settings → Data → Clear all data, or uninstall the app.

For more, see the [FAQ](/explore/faq) and [How to use](/explore/how-to-use). For how data is handled, see the [privacy policy](/privacy).
