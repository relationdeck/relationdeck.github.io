# About RelationDeck

RelationDeck is a deck of conversation cards, made into a phone app.

We noticed that a lot of conversations stop at "how have you been?" — not because there's nothing to say, but because nobody has a good way in. A good question is often that way in.

## What's inside

**12 relationship decks.** Thirty cards each, 360 cards in all:

- Yourself and social: Self Reflection, Icebreaker, Friends
- Love: Dating, Couples, Life Together
- Family: Talk to Parents, Talk to Kids, Siblings
- Work: Workplace, Managing Up, 1-on-1

Each relationship is also clear about who is asking whom: a grown child asking their parents, or a parent asking a child; someone asking their manager, or a manager asking their report.

**3 levels of depth.** Every card is marked Light, Deeper or Deepest. Cards are dealt from light to deep, so you warm up first and go further as you go.

**How you draw.** Put the phone on the table, tap to flip a card, take turns answering, and swipe left for the next one. No repeats in a round — the deck reshuffles once you've been through it all.

**After the conversation.** Save cards you want to come back to; leave a note on any question and tag who you talked with; under Records, look back at which relationships you talked about over the last 30 days, how deep you went, and who you haven't talked with in a while, plus your streak and a conversation calendar. You can also turn on a daily reminder to get one question a day.

**Two languages.** The interface and every question are available in Traditional Chinese and English.

## Privacy

The app needs no account. Saved cards, notes, people and history are stored only on your device and are not uploaded to any server by default.
