# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 21, 2026

This policy explains how RelationDeck ("we") handles your data in its iOS and Android apps (the "App") and on its website, www.relationdeck.com (the "Website").

This English version is provided for convenience. If it differs from the [Traditional Chinese version](pathname:///privacy), the Chinese version prevails.

## 1. Summary

- The App requires no account and never asks for your name, email or phone number.
- What you create in the App (saved cards, notes, people, draw history and settings) is stored only on your device and, by default, is never uploaded to our servers or any third party's.
- The App contains no ads, analytics or tracking tools.
- We do not sell, rent or share any user data.

## 2. Data stored on your device

When you use the App, the following data is stored in a local database and in the App's settings on your device:

| Data | What it contains |
|---|---|
| Saved cards | The cards you saved, and when each card was last drawn |
| Notes | The text you write on a card, when it was created and edited, and the person it is tagged with |
| People | The names and memos you enter yourself |
| Draw history | The date and time each card was turned over, used for your streak and conversation calendar |
| Settings | The interface language, and the daily reminder's on/off switch, time and deck |

This data is used by the App only to provide the features above. We cannot access, read or back up this data, so we cannot restore it for you either.

"People" is text you type yourself and may include other people's names or descriptions of them. It too stays only on your device; please use your own judgement about what you enter.

## 3. The App's network connections

The App connects to the internet in only one situation, only to our own server, and never sends anything you have created in the App:

**Card content updates.** At most once every 24 hours (or when you manually check for updates in Settings), the App downloads the latest card questions from `api.relationdeck.com`. This request contains no account, device identifier or user data; it carries a single identifier shared by every copy of the App, used only to tell that the request comes from the App, which cannot identify you. As with any network connection, the server technically receives your IP address and standard connection information; we do not use it to identify you.

The App's fonts are built into the App and are not downloaded from Google or any other third party.

Without a network connection, the App uses its built-in card content and every feature works as usual.

## 4. Notifications and permissions

**Daily reminder** is off by default. The App asks for notification permission only when you turn it on in Settings. Reminders are scheduled and delivered locally by your device, without any push server.

The Android version also uses the "run at startup" permission (`RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED`) to restore scheduled reminders after the device restarts.

**Sharing a card** turns the question into an image on your device. The image contains only the question, the deck name and the App's name — never your notes, people or history. When you tap Share, the image is handed to the system share sheet, and you decide where it goes. On iOS, if you choose "Save Image" in the share sheet, the system asks whether to allow adding to Photos; the App can only add the image to your library and cannot read your photos.

The App does not access your contacts, location, microphone or camera, and does not read your photos.

## 5. Deleting data

- You can delete individual notes or unsave cards in the App at any time.
- Settings → Data → Clear all data deletes all saved cards, notes, people and draw history at once. Settings such as language and reminders are kept.
- Uninstalling the App deletes all App data stored on that device.

Because none of your data is on our servers, there is no separate deletion request to make.

## 6. Website

The Website is a static site with no account registration. When you browse it, the following third-party services may collect data:

- **Google Tag Manager / Google Analytics**: used to understand how the site is visited, such as which pages are viewed and where visitors come from. This data is handled by Google under its [privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Google Fonts**: the Website's fonts are served from Google's servers.
- **Substack**: the newsletter sign-up form on the home page is provided by Substack. The email you enter goes directly to Substack and is handled under the [Substack privacy policy](https://substack.com/privacy).

You can block cookies in your browser settings, or use the [Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

## 7. Children's privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children. The App does not ask for personal data and uploads nothing by default; all content stays on the user's own device.

## 8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be announced in the App or on the Website.
