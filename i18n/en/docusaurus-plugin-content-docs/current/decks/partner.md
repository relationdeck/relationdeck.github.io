# Couples

**Couples** · 30 cards (10 Light, 10 Deeper, 10 Deepest)

Not just the day. What's underneath it. Dreams, fears and gratitude — the things that make it real.

## Good for

- Weekend evenings or anniversaries
- Traveling together, or other relaxed times
- When you feel like all you've talked about lately is day-to-day stuff

## Sample questions

### Light

- What's your favorite memory of us?
- What are three things you love about me?

### Deeper

- What's the most moving thing I've ever done for you?
- How have you changed since we've been together?

### Deepest

- What's something you've wanted to tell me but haven't?
- What do you think we most need to work on together?

## Tips

- Deepest questions work best when it's quiet and you're not in a hurry.
- When listening, don't rush to explain or push back. Wait until your partner has finished before responding.
- If the conversation gets uncomfortable, it's fine to pause and switch to a Light card.
