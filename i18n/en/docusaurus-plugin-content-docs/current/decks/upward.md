# Managing Up

**Managing Up** · 30 cards (10 Light, 10 Deeper, 10 Deepest)

Not just reporting. Knowing who leads you. Know your manager or mentor as a person — the work clicks, and growth finds a direction.

In this deck, you ask your manager or mentor. If you're a manager who wants to talk one-on-one with your direct reports, use [1-on-1](./subordinate.md).

## Good for

- One-on-one meetings (1-on-1s)
- When you've just gotten a new manager or joined a new team
- Coffee or lunch with a mentor

## Sample questions

### Light

- How did you get to where you are now?
- When you first became a manager, what took the most getting used to?

### Deeper

- Where would you most like to see me grow?
- What did the best manager you ever had do right?

### Deepest

- What do you think I still need to reach the next level?
- When we disagree, how would you like me to raise it?

## Tips

- Starting with questions about your manager's own experience feels more natural than asking for feedback right away.
- Pick just one or two questions per 1-on-1. There's no need to turn the meeting into a Q&A.
- Write down the advice you get in your notes, and look it over before your next 1-on-1 to see how much you've acted on.
