# Siblings

**Siblings** · 30 cards (10 Light, 10 Deeper, 10 Deepest)

Same home, different memories. Talk about growing up together, and who you each grew up to be.

## Good for

- Holidays back home, when brothers and sisters are finally together
- On a trip together, or on the way back to your hometown
- Before discussing things about your parents, take time to talk about each other

## Sample questions

### Light

- What did we fight about most as kids?
- What's the naughtiest thing you remember us doing together?

### Deeper

- How do you think your place in the family — oldest, middle, youngest — shaped who you are?
- How do you think we've changed toward each other since we grew up?

### Deepest

- Is there something from when we were kids you've always wanted to apologize or say thank you for?
- As our parents get older, how would you like us to face it together?

## Tips

- Two people often remember the same event differently. That's the fun part, so there's no need to argue about who's right.
- When talking about your parents, start with your own feelings so it doesn't turn into criticizing them together.
- Siblings who don't live together can draw cards together over a video call.
