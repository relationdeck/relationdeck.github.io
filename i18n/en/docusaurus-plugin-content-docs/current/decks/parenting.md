# Talk to Kids

**Talk to Kids** · 30 cards (10 Light, 10 Deeper, 10 Deepest)

More than “were you good today?” Questions a kid can answer, so you hear what they're really thinking.

This deck is for moms and dads to ask their kids, in words kids understand.

## Good for

- Winding down at bedtime or after a bath
- On the way to or from school
- At the dinner table, with the whole family taking turns

## Sample questions

### Light

- What was the best moment of your day today?
- If you could have one superpower, which would you pick, and why?

### Deeper

- Has anything made you really sad or angry lately?
- When things get hard, how would you like me to help?

### Deepest

- Is there anything you're worried I'd be angry about if you told me?
- What's something you'd like to hear me say more often?

## Tips

- With younger kids, an adult can read the question, and the kids can ask the adults the same question back.
- When kids tell you about something that made them sad or angry, say "Thank you for telling me" first, then talk about what to do.
- Deepest questions work best one-on-one, not in front of brothers and sisters.
