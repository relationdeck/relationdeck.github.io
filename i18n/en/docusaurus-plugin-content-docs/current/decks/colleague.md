# Workplace

**Workplace** · 30 cards (10 Light, 10 Deeper, 10 Deepest)

Not just working together. Understanding each other. See the person, and the work gets easier.

## Good for

- Team lunches or coffee breaks
- A new team member's first week
- Team-building events, or a retrospective after a project wraps up

## Sample questions

### Light

- What's the best thing you've picked up since joining this team?
- What's a work habit you've found really effective?

### Deeper

- What do you think is our team's biggest strength?
- What kind of collaboration makes you most productive?

### Deepest

- If you could change one thing about this workplace, what would it be?
- What work challenge have you wanted to take on but haven't?

## Tips

- The questions are mostly about work, with a few about interests outside it. That makes the deck a good fit when you want to know colleagues better without getting too personal.
- This isn't a performance review. Whoever is leading can answer first to take the pressure off.
- Deepest questions touch on misunderstandings, stress, and times you wanted to give up. They're best for colleagues who have worked together for a while and trust each other.
