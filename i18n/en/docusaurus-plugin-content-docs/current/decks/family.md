# Talk to Parents

**Talk to Parents** · 30 cards (10 Light, 10 Deeper, 10 Deepest)

Less politeness. More listening. Every generation has a story worth saying out loud.

This deck is for grown children to ask their mom and dad. To talk with your kids, use [Talk to Kids](./parenting.md); to talk with your brothers and sisters, use [Siblings](./sibling.md).

## Good for

- Lunar New Year and family dinners
- Visiting home for a meal or a walk with your parents
- Looking through old photos with older family members

## Sample questions

### Light

- What was home like when you were a kid?
- How did you two meet?

### Deeper

- What was your biggest dream when you were young?
- What changed most for you after becoming a parent?

### Deepest

- What's something about you you wish I knew?
- How would you like me to be there as you get older?

## Tips

- Starting with stories from your parents' younger days is usually easier than talking about feelings right away.
- The stories older family members tell are worth keeping. Write them in a note and tag who told them.
- You don't have to ask every Deepest question at once. Save them for when it's just the two of you.
