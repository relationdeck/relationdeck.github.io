# 1-on-1

**1-on-1** · 30 cards (10 Light, 10 Deeper, 10 Deepest)

Not managing. Building trust. One honest conversation at a time, until moving forward feels safe.

## Good for

- One-on-one meetings (1-on-1s)
- When a new team member hits one month or one quarter
- Before discussing annual goals

## Sample questions

### Light

- What have you worked on lately that you're proud of?

### Deeper

- What do you think I do best as your manager?
- How does your workload actually feel right now?

### Deepest

- What do you wish I understood better about what you need?
- What support or resources are you missing right now?

## Tips

- This deck is for managers to ask their direct reports. Once you've asked, your job is to listen closely.
- Write the answers as notes and tag the person. Before your next 1-on-1, open their page under People to see what you talked about last time.
- Deepest questions involve feedback and needs, so use them once you've built some trust.
