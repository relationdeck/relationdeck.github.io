# Friends

**Friends** · 30 cards (10 Light, 10 Deeper, 10 Deepest)

One good question beats three hours of chit-chat. New stories from old friends, and new friends who feel close fast.

## Good for

- Dinners, or evenings on a trip
- Catching up with friends you haven't seen in a long time
- Small get-togethers of three to six people

## Sample questions

### Light

- What's something that's made you feel proud lately?

### Deeper

- What's something you've wanted to do but keep putting off?
- What's changed about you over the past year?

### Deepest

- What part of your life needs more of your attention right now?
- What dream would you change your life for?

## Tips

- Having everyone answer the same question usually goes deeper than one question per person.
- To keep everyone's answers, write several notes on the same card and tag each one with who said it.
- If there are a lot of new faces, warm up with a few Icebreaker cards before switching to this deck.
