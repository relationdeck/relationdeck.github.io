# Icebreaker

**Icebreaker** · 30 cards (15 Light, 15 Deeper)

First meetings, beyond small talk. The right question starts a real conversation — and finds what you share.

## Good for

- First meetings with new classmates, roommates, or club members
- Icebreakers at the start of an event or workshop
- When a friend brings someone new to a get-together

## Sample questions

### Light

- What's the best movie or show you've watched lately?
- If you could instantly master one skill, what would it be?

### Deeper

- If you could have dinner with anyone, who would it be?
- What's your simplest definition of happiness?

## Tips

- This deck has only two levels, Light and Deeper, which suits people who don't know each other well yet.
- Take turns drawing. After you answer, ask the other person the same question, and the conversation keeps going on its own.
- Skip any question you don't want to answer. Just swipe left or tap **Next card**.
