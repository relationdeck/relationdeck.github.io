# Life Together

**Life Together** · 30 cards (10 Light, 10 Deeper, 10 Deepest)

Love is the easy part. Living together takes talking. Money, chores, family and the future — the everyday things, out on the table.

[Couples](./partner.md) is about your relationship; this deck is about the practical side of living together. Married or not, it's for anyone who lives together or plans to.

## Good for

- Around the time you move in together or get married
- When life needs adjusting, like a move or a job change
- At the end of the year, when you're planning the next one together

## Sample questions

### Light

- Which of my habits drives you up the wall?
- What's your favorite way to unwind together in the evening?

### Deeper

- Do you think the way we split the housework is fair?
- When it comes to whose family we spend the holidays with, what do you really think?

### Deepest

- Is there anything about our money that makes you uneasy?
- As both our parents get older, how would you like us to handle it?

## Tips

- These questions can stir up emotions, so pick a time when neither of you is tired.
- The goal isn't to make a decision on the spot, but to understand what matters to the other person.
- When a question leads to a conclusion, write it in your notes and look back later to see whether you followed through.
