# Self Reflection

**Self Reflection** · 30 cards (10 Light, 10 Deeper, 10 Deepest)

Sit with yourself, and ask. Find your feelings, your direction, and yourself again.

## Good for

- Quiet moments alone, before bed or on your commute
- When you want to sort through recent feelings or your direction
- When you want to journal but don't know where to start

## Sample questions

### Light

- What was one moment today that felt good?
- What's the best thing you've bought yourself lately?

### Deeper

- What moment keeps replaying in your head?
- What do you owe yourself a thank-you for?

### Deepest

- How much of what you're working toward do you actually want?
- What part of yourself are you most afraid people will see?

## Tips

- This deck is for you. You don't need to say your answers out loud; you can write them straight into the card's notes.
- When you draw the same card again some time later, look back at your last note. You'll often see how you've changed.
- Under **Settings → Daily reminder**, set the topic to Self Reflection to get one question at the same time every day.
