# Dating

**Dating** · 30 cards (10 Light, 10 Deeper, 10 Deepest)

Not sure what to say? Let a card speak first, and get closer question by question.

## Good for

- The first few dates
- Getting to know someone you're interested in
- Dinners where you want to talk about more than work and the weather

## Sample questions

### Light

- What have you been obsessed with lately?
- What's the restaurant you keep going back to?

### Deeper

- What's the last thing that really moved you?
- What do people usually get wrong about you at first?

### Deepest

- What does an ordinary good day together look like?
- When do you feel loneliest?

## Tips

- Start with Light questions, and move on to Deeper and Deepest once you're both comfortable.
- Sharing specific details brings you closer than giving the "right" answer.
- Jot down the small things the other person mentions in your notes, so you'll have something to pick up on next time.
