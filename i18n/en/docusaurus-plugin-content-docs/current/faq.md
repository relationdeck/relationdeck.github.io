# FAQ

## Do I need to sign up for an account?

No. Download the app, open it, and start.

## Where are my notes and records stored?

Your saved cards, notes, people, and draw history are stored only on your phone and are not uploaded to any server by default. For details, see the [Privacy Policy](/privacy).

## If I switch phones or delete the app, is my data still there?

No. Because your data lives only on your phone, there is currently no cloud sync or backup. Uninstalling the app deletes all of your data.

## Can I use it offline?

Yes. All cards are built into the app, so you can draw cards and write notes without an internet connection. When you're online, the app occasionally downloads new question content, which takes effect the next time you open the app. You can also check manually under **Settings → Check for updates**.

## When a group plays together, how do I record everyone's answers?

A card can have more than one note. Write one note per person and pick the matching person in each note. Later, go to **Records → People** and tap someone to see all of their answers.

## Will I draw the same card twice?

Not in the same round. After you've drawn all 30 cards in a deck, you can shuffle again. Closing and reopening the app also starts a new round.

## Can I draw only cards of a certain level?

There's no level filter, and you don't need one. Each deck deals Light questions first, then Deeper, and finally Deepest, in random order within each level. To skip a card, just swipe left; cards you don't flip aren't counted in your records.

## Does Review score my relationships?

No. Review only describes what you've talked about in the last 30 days: which relationships come up most, how deep the conversations usually go, who you haven't talked to in a while, and how many questions are left in the decks you've started. There are no scores or rankings, and no comparison with last month.

## How is the streak counted?

A day counts if you flip at least one card that day. Missing a day resets the streak. If you drew yesterday but haven't drawn yet today, you'll see "Not drawn today," and your streak won't reset yet.

## The daily reminder isn't showing up?

Check the following:

- **Settings → Daily reminder** is turned on.
- RelationDeck is allowed to send notifications in your phone's system settings.
- If you've already drawn a card that day, you won't get a reminder.

## I don't want to see someone in my People list anymore?

In **Records → People**, edit that person and choose **Remove partner**. They will no longer appear in the list, but notes tagged with them are kept and shown as "No one in particular."

## How do I clear all my data?

Go to **Settings → Data → Clear all data** to delete every saved card, note, person, and draw record at once. This cannot be undone.
