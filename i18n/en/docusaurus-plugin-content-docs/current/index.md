# Card Resources

This section covers how to use RelationDeck, an introduction to each deck, and frequently asked questions.

## Start here

- [How to use](./how-to-use.md): drawing cards, saving, notes, people, review, and the daily reminder.
- [FAQ](./faq.md): where your data is stored, whether you need an internet connection, and how to record answers from several people.

## The twelve decks

The app includes 12 decks of 30 cards each, 360 cards in total. Each card has one of three levels: Light, Deeper, and Deepest. Icebreaker has only Light and Deeper, because the deepest questions are for people who already know each other.

| Deck | For | In a sentence |
|---|---|---|
| [Self Reflection](./decks/reflection.md) | Yourself | Sit with yourself, and ask. |
| [Icebreaker](./decks/stranger.md) | People you've just met | First meetings, beyond small talk. |
| [Friends](./decks/friend.md) | Friends | One good question beats three hours of chit-chat. |
| [Dating](./decks/dating.md) | Someone you're dating | Not sure what to say? |
| [Couples](./decks/partner.md) | Partners | Not just the day. What's underneath it. |
| [Life Together](./decks/household.md) | Couples who live together or are married | Love is the easy part. Living together takes talking. |
| [Talk to Parents](./decks/family.md) | Adult children asking their parents | Less politeness. More listening. |
| [Talk to Kids](./decks/parenting.md) | Parents asking their kids | More than “were you good today?” |
| [Siblings](./decks/sibling.md) | Brothers and sisters | Same home, different memories. |
| [Workplace](./decks/colleague.md) | Colleagues | Not just working together. Understanding each other. |
| [Managing Up](./decks/upward.md) | Your manager or mentor | Not just reporting. Knowing who leads you. |
| [1-on-1](./decks/subordinate.md) | Your direct reports (1-on-1) | Not managing. Building trust. |
