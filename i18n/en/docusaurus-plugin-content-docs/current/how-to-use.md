# How to Use

The app has four tabs at the bottom: **Draw**, **Saved**, **Records**, and **Settings**.

## Draw

1. On the **Draw** tab, pick a deck.
2. Tap the back of the card to flip it, read the question aloud, and take turns answering.
3. Swipe left for the next card, or right to go back to the previous one. When the phone is lying flat on the table, you can also just tap **Next card**.

Each deck deals Light questions first, then Deeper, and finally Deepest, in random order within each level. You don't need to pick questions at the start: draw them in order, and the conversation will naturally get deeper. Each card also shows its level in the corner.

The shuffle button in the top right reshuffles the cards you haven't drawn yet (still from light to deep). Cards you've already drawn are still there if you swipe back.

You won't get the same question twice in one round. After you've drawn all 30 cards, you can shuffle again for another round or pick a different deck. Closing and reopening the app also starts a new round.

A card only counts as drawn once it's flipped: cards you swipe away without flipping are not recorded.

### A few tips for better conversations

- Lay the phone flat in the middle of the table so everyone can see the question.
- Whoever reads the question doesn't have to answer first; they can pick someone to start.
- Skip any question you don't want to answer. No reason needed.
- Listeners don't need to rush to give advice. Asking "And then what?" usually works better than advice.

## Saved

After flipping a card, tap the bookmark to add it to the **Saved** tab. It's a good place for questions you want to come back to another day, or talk about with someone else.

## Notes

After flipping a card, tap **Write a note**, or open the card's detail page, to write a note for that card. A card can have more than one note: if three people answer the same question, write three notes and tag each one with who said it.

## Records

The top of the **Records** tab shows your streak: a day counts if you flip at least one card that day. If you drew yesterday but haven't drawn yet today, you'll see "Not drawn today," and your streak won't reset.

Below that, you can switch between four views:

- **Review**: a summary of your conversations over the last 30 days. See below.
- **Notes**: a timeline of all your notes.
- **People**: the people you talk with often. See below.
- **Calendar**: how many cards you drew each day. Tap a day to see which cards you drew.

### Review

Review summarizes your conversations from the last 30 days:

- **Your last 30 days**: which area you've talked about most lately (Yourself, Social, Love, Family, or Work), how many cards you drew in each, and how deep those conversations usually went. It also points out which area has been quiet lately.
- **Deep moments**: notes you've recently written on Deepest cards.
- **Not talked to in a while**: people with no new notes for more than 30 days.
- **Questions still waiting**: how many questions are left in the decks you've started.

Review only describes; it doesn't give scores or compare with last month. It's empty right after you install the app, and fills in once you've drawn a few cards and written a few notes.

### People

**People** are the people you talk with often, such as a partner, family member, friend, or manager. When writing a note, you can choose who the conversation was with, or add someone new right from the menu.

Tap a person to see every note from your conversations together, newest first. Look through them before you next meet, and you'll know where you left off.

If you no longer need someone in the list, choose **Remove partner** on their edit page. Notes tagged with them are kept and shown as "No one in particular."

## Daily reminder

Turn it on under **Settings → Daily reminder** to get a notification at a set time each day. The notification contains a question, and tapping it takes you straight to that card.

- The default time is 8 PM, and you can change it.
- For the topic, you can choose a specific deck or **Random**.
- Cards you haven't drawn yet are picked first.
- If you've already drawn a card that day, you won't get a reminder.

The reminder is off by default. You'll be asked for notification permission only when you turn it on.

## Language

Under **Settings → Language**, you can choose System, 繁體中文, or English. The interface and all questions switch together.
